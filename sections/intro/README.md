# Introduction

Before you start the first module, take ten minutes for this introduction. It tells you what this course trains you to do, how to get your machine ready, and who builds the course. You will not touch Istio here, but these three short pages make every page after them easier to follow.

The introduction has three pages, and they are best read in the order they come. **Welcome To The Course** explains who the course is for, what you can do at the end, how the course is laid out, and how to read a page. **Get Your Machine Ready** lists the tools you need, the `astrona` commands you will use every day, and what to do when a start goes wrong. **How This Course Is Made** explains how the content is written and checked, who maintains it, and how to report a mistake.

When you are done, the course starts with finding configuration errors with `istioctl analyze`.
